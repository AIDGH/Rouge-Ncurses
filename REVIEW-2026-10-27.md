# Rouge-Ncurses — local repair and visual pass

Base: `9a71f87` (2025-03-23). Repairs were ported from the earlier `fop2024-project-AIDGH` review using a three-way comparison, resolving overlaps to retain this edition's character selection, mouse interaction, Timmy, room types, audio and shorter floor count.

## Reliability

- Room pointers now own zero-initialized heap storage, including secret and battle rooms. Generation no longer leaves pointers to stack-local rooms.
- Seven map slots include temporary battle floor 6. Movement checks, viewport limits and staircase bounds prevent invalid indexing. Floor traversal no longer recursively grows the call stack; returning from battles restores room identity. Returning to an earlier floor also restores its room count.
- Bounded generation retries, connected-route fallback, correctly sized door buffers and counted placements preserve playable generation across supported terminal sizes.
- Dropped projectiles grow room inventory and retain complete weapon metadata. Corrected trap visibility and game-count expressions were ported.
- Version-2 full saves validate inputs, preserve shield/item/ammo state and retain a previous `.bak` with atomic replacement. Automatic saves now keep the world instead of replacing it with a profile-only header; saves in temporary battle rooms wait until return. Profile listing excludes `.bak` files.
- Main-menu keyboard Enter and mouse click selection are deterministic. Music selection handles an empty directory or cancellation without assigning a null track. Escape also backs out of inventory. Repeated runs release room storage and reused decoded effects.
- Native build objects are separate from old Linux objects. Launcher resets the terminal, records errors and stays open after failure. Escape key timeout reduced to 25 ms.

## Visual changes

- New castle ASCII title with a restrained cyan, gold, violet and stone palette, shared by the menus and gameplay.
- Continuous thin room outlines, quieter passage texture, consistent text-style item symbols rather than variable-width coloured emoji.
- Fixed-width health/shield/food meters with numeric values, weapon stock/rank, gold, keys and timed effects.
- Timmy remains the guide, now drawn as an outlined lantern spirit. Original dragon and other large ASCII illustrations are preserved.
- H opens a framed field guide and symbol legend; the permanent shortcut hint is on the bottom border.
